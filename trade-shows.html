Beyond the Booth — Buyer Meetings in Their Own Offices

Not every buyer travels to a show — so we travel instead. When a supermarket group, importer or retail chain wants to evaluate a China drinkware supplier before committing to a container, we come to their office and their showroom: samples in the bag, price tiers and spec sheets on the table, packaging and barcode options settled on the spot. Two of those visits are below — a supermarket chain's buying team in Jakarta, and a buyer's own office and showroom in Dubai.

What we bring to a buyer's office

  • Physical samples — the actual tumbler, bottle or flask a buyer would order, printed with a real brand logo.
  • Costed price tiers by quantity, capacity and packaging, so MOQ and target price can be settled in one sitting.
  • Customisation options — logo printing, laser engraving, powder-coating and gradient finishes.
  • Retail-ready packaging — colour boxes, gift sets, UPC/EAN barcodes and private-label artwork.
  • Compliance files — FDA / LFGB food-contact reports, ISO 9001, SGS / TUV and BSCI.

Why we fly out to a buyer's own market

  • The decision-makers, in person — the owner or buyer who signs the order, not a reply-to address.
  • Our range in the room — bottles, tumblers and flasks shown and compared in person, not a catalogue PDF.
  • The market seen first-hand — which capacities, finishes and price points actually move on shelves in their market.
  • Next steps agreed before we fly home — samples, trial order and delivery schedule fixed in the room.

Why Meeting Buyers in Person Matters

You see the real productLids, threads, powder-coating, wall thickness and finish are judged in the hand — not in a catalogue photo. At the booth you can open, twist, pour and compare every model we make.
You meet the actual makerTrading companies send salespeople; we send the factory team. Buyers can ask about materials (304 / 316 stainless, Tritan, PP), capacity, insulation performance and customisation directly.
Deals move fasterA five-minute conversation at a show replaces three weeks of emails. MOQ, colourways, packaging and target price are agreed on the spot, and samples go out the week after.
Trust is verified, not claimedAnyone can write "20 years' experience" on a website. Standing at our booth with our own products, our own staff and our own clients is proof that cannot be faked.
Long-term partners start hereMany of our current wholesale and private-label customers first met us at an exhibition, handled one tumbler, and placed their first trial order from the show floor.
After the show, we keep shippingThe booth is the beginning. Production, AQL inspection, OEM packaging and FOB shipping from Ningbo continue long after the expo halls close.

What We Bring to Every Show

Our booth is a compact version of our factory floor — the same ranges, the same finishes, the same customisation options our production lines run every week.

  • Full drinkware range: insulated bottles (12oz–2L), stainless steel tumblers (including 20oz/30oz/40oz handled tumblers), travel mugs, vacuum flasks, sport & shaker bottles, kids bottles, tea infuser bottles, wine flasks and beer growlers.
  • Live customisation samples: logo printing, laser engraving, UV & water-transfer printing, powder-coating and gradient finishes — so buyers can feel what their own brand would look like.
  • Retail-ready packaging: colour boxes, gift sets, barcode (UPC/EAN) placement and private-label artwork for supermarket and retail shelves.
  • Compliance documents on request: FDA / LFGB food-contact reports, ISO 9001, SGS/TUV and BSCI social-compliance certificates.
  • A buying-office hat too: beyond drinkware, our China buying office helps retail chains source other categories, consolidate suppliers into one container and inspect quality (AQL 2.5).
