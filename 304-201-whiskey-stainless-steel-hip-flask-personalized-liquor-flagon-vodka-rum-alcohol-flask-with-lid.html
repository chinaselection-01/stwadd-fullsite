Product Description

This is the classic flat hip flask form, built for a pocket: pressed 18/8 (304) stainless steel with a double-wall vacuum layer and a copper-plated reflective surface, so a drink holds its temperature for 6-12 hours cold and 3-6 hours hot. The flat profile is what makes it a hip flask rather than a bottle — it sits flat in a jacket pocket or a rucksack and does not roll.

The body is food-contact tested to US FDA 21 CFR and EU LFGB (EN 1186) migration requirements. The screw cap uses a silicone gasket, chosen so that it stays stable in contact with high-proof spirits and can be replaced as a spare rather than discarded with the flask. Every sealing part is a stocked spare, which is what keeps the model in your range after the first shipment.

Personalisation is the point of this shape. The flat front panel takes a laser-engraved name, a date, a crest or a logo with very little distortion, which is what makes it work as a gift, a corporate order or a wedding favour. Silk screen, UV print and heat transfer are also available for branded runs, and spray paint or powder coating handles a colour-matched range. MOQ starts at 500 pieces on the existing shape; a new shape with new tooling needs 3,000 pieces, and the mould is yours once paid for.

Samples of the stock model ship in 1-3 days and custom samples in 7-10 days, and the sample itself is free — you pay only the courier. Production runs 25-30 days after artwork and sample approval, with AQL 2.5 inspection before the container is sealed. We can also quote the retail gift box, which has its own minimum order quantity and is worth asking for as a separate number.

STWADD is a factory-backed drinkware supply company in Yongkang, Zhejiang: we produce to your specification at audited partner factories we qualify and work with directly, with our own QC team on the line. We publish the original third-party food-contact reports for the models we make, including an SGS report where the Manufacturer field is STWADD itself.
