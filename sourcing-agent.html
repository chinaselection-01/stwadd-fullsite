Frequently Asked Questions

What is a China buying office / sourcing agent?
A China buying office (also called a sourcing agent) is a local team in China that represents an overseas buyer. It finds suppliers, negotiates price, inspects quality, consolidates goods from multiple factories into one shipment, handles private-label packaging, and arranges freight to your port. STWADD runs such a buying office for supermarket chains and retail buyers — with one difference: we are also a drinkware factory, so your bottles and tumblers can be supplied directly from our own lines at factory-bottom cost.
How can a China sourcing agent help my supermarket chain?
A China sourcing agent helps a supermarket chain by: (1) finding vetted factories for the exact products you need across categories; (2) negotiating the best ex-factory price and protecting you from trading-company markup; (3) consolidating small orders from many suppliers into one container to save freight; (4) inspecting every batch (AQL 2.5) and auditing factories for social compliance and capacity; (5) handling private-label packaging, barcodes and documentation; (6) arranging ocean freight and customs paperwork to your destination port. STWADD does all of this and also supplies drinkware from its own factory.
Do you consolidate multiple suppliers into one container (consolidation shipping)?
Yes. Container consolidation is a core service of our China buying office. When your supermarket sources from several Chinese factories, we receive the goods at our warehouse, inspect and repack them, and load everything into one 20ft, 40ft or 40HQ container. This turns many small supplier shipments into a single consolidated container, cutting international freight cost dramatically and simplifying customs clearance at your port.
What are your sourcing agent fees / commission?
STWADD charges a transparent 3-5% commission billed against the factory invoice — far below the 5-15% (or hidden 15-30% trading-company markup) typical of many China sourcing agents. There is no separate hidden margin. Sample coordination, pre-shipment AQL inspection and consolidation warehousing are included. A minimum project value (around US$20,000 per consolidated container) keeps our team focused on serious supermarket buyers.
Are you a sourcing agent or a factory?
Both. STWADD is a drinkware factory (OEM/ODM manufacturer of insulated bottles, flasks, tumblers and mugs in Yongkang, China) AND a China buying office / sourcing agent for retail chains. For drinkware, we supply you directly from our own lines at factory-bottom price. For everything else your supermarket needs, our buying office sources from vetted external suppliers, consolidates, inspects and ships. This combination — factory + agent — is what most pure sourcing agents cannot offer.
Do you provide quality inspection and factory audits in China?
Yes. Our buying office performs in-house quality inspection on every order (pre-production sample approval, and pre-shipment AQL 2.5 inspection with a downloadable report and packaging photos before the container loads). We also run annual factory audits for any external supplier we recommend, covering fire safety, social compliance and production capacity. Third-party inspection (SGS / BV) can be arranged on request.
Can you do private label / OEM packaging for our supermarket brand?
Yes. As both a factory and a buying office, STWADD handles private-label and OEM packaging for retail chains: custom logo application, retail-ready color boxes, barcode (UPC/EAN) placement, and gift-set assembly. Your supermarket's own brand goes on the product and packaging; our name stays off it. NDA and design confidentiality are standard.
Beyond drinkware, what products can your China buying office source?
Our buying office sources the non-food general-merchandise categories that fill supermarket, convenience, dollar and discount-store shelves — plus drinkware, which we supply directly from our own factory. Core categories include: drinkware & bottles (insulated water bottles, stainless steel tumblers, vacuum flasks); kitchenware & dining (food storage, lunch boxes, utensils, cookware); home storage & organization; cleaning & household care; pet supplies (cat trees, pet beds, feeding bowls); toys, kids & baby; home textiles & bedding; beauty & personal-care tools; garden & outdoor; electronics & lighting accessories; hardware, tools & auto accessories; and seasonal, party & hygiene products. For each category we shortlist 5-10 vetted factories, quote per-unit and per-container pricing, and consolidate mixed-category orders into one shipment. We focus on stable-demand, long-shelf-life, compliant general merchandise rather than fresh or perishable food.
How do you arrange shipping from China to our port?
After consolidation and inspection at our warehouse, STWADD arranges ocean freight (FCL consolidation) and prepares the full document set — commercial invoice, packing list, bill of lading, and certificates needed for your destination (e.g. FDA/LFGB declarations for food-contact items). We support FOB, EXW and can assist with freight forwarding and destination-port customs clearance. Typical lead time: 10 days for in-stock items, 30-35 days for production runs, 5-7 days for consolidated container loading from Ningbo or Yiwu.

We focus on supermarket and retail-chain buyers with a minimum consolidated project value of around US$20,000 per container. This filter keeps our buying-office team dedicated to serious wholesale programs rather than one-off small parcels. For drinkware alone, our factory MOQ starts from about 500 pieces and is negotiable.
Do you source food, fresh or perishable products?
We focus on non-food general merchandise and drinkware for retail chains.
