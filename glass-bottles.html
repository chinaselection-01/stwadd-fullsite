Frequently Asked Questions

Do glass bottles come with a protective sleeve?

Yes, most glass bottles ship with a removable silicone sleeve for grip and drop protection.

Can glass bottles hold hot drinks?

Borosilicate glass models are thermal-shock resistant and safe for hot beverages; sleeve models add insulation.
