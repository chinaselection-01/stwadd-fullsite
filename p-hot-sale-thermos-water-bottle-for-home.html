Product Overview

The "Hot Sale Thermos Water Bottle for Home" by STWADD is a high-quality thermos water bottle designed for various indoor and outdoor applications.

Product Features

This thermos water bottle has a capacity of 500ml, features a LED temperature display, and can keep liquids hot or cold for 6-12 hours.

Product Value

The thermos water bottle comes with a gift box, making it an ideal gift option. It is certified with MSDS report and UN38.3 Test report, ensuring quality and safety.

Product Advantages

The factory offers OEM and ODM one-stop service, material customization, free inspection, and trade assurance for secure orders.

Application Scenarios

The thermos water bottle is suitable for use in various settings such as offices, bedrooms, kitchens, living rooms, indoor, outdoor, dining rooms, and more.
