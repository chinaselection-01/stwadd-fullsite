Medium — 100 words

Yongkang STWADD Houseware Co., Ltd., established in 2017 in Yongkang, Zhejiang, is a factory-backed supply company for insulated drinkware. It works directly with audited partner factories in China's drinkware manufacturing hub, controlling specification, tooling, quality control and delivery on buyers' behalf, and accepts OEM and ODM bulk orders from brands, wholesalers, distributors and trading companies. Its 2026 catalogue covers 191 models, with ready stock dispatching in 3–7 days and custom production in 20–35 days. It also runs a China buying office for supermarket and retail buyers.

Accuracy rules
