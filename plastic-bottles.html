Frequently Asked Questions

Are your plastic bottles BPA-free?

All our plastic bottles are BPA-free; Tritan models meet Eastman food-contact standards and are odor- and stain-resistant.

We produce up to 2L sports and gym bottles with carry straps and times-to-drink markers.

Yes, our PET and Tritan bottles are recyclable; we also offer recycled-material options on request.
