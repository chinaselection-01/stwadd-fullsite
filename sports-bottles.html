Frequently Asked Questions

Do you make shaker bottles?

Yes — we produce shaker bottles with mixing balls and measuring marks for protein and pre-workout use.

Are sports bottles leak-proof?

All sport caps use a locking flip or push-pull mechanism tested for leak resistance during transport.

What capacities suit gym use?

500ml-1L are most popular for training; we also make 1.5L-2L hydration bottles.
