We Are a Factory, Not a Trading Company

STWADD is a manufacturer (factory), not a trading company. We run our own production lines in Yongkang with automated equipment and in-house mold making, logo printing (laser engraving, screen print, water transfer) and assembly. Being the factory means we control quality, lead time and cost — and can pass the cheapest factory-direct prices to buyers with no middleman markup.

What "cheap East factory" means for your order

  • No trader commission — you buy at factory-bottom cost.
  • Located in Yongkang, Zhejiang, the drinkware manufacturing cluster of East Asia, with mature local supply chains that keep material and labor costs low.
  • Automated lines (including robotic assembly) reduce labor cost and stabilize delivery.
  • Price scales with volume — the larger the order, the lower the unit cost.
