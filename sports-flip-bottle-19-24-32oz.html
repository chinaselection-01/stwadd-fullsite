Why buyers are looking at this bottle

Retail buyers increasingly ask for a sports bottle that is simple to use, easy to clean and free of the failure points that generate returns — a lid that leaks in a bag, a mechanism that breaks, a cap that traps mould. This design answers those requests with fewer moving parts: one flip lid, one straw, one silicone gasket.

The liner is 304 stainless steel; the lid, straw and cap are PP, with a silicone sealing ring. Three capacities and a wide colour range make it easy to build a family of SKUs under one tooling.

Fewer parts to cleanFlip lid, straw and gasket can be separated and washed, instead of a fixed multi-piece spout.
304 linerStainless inner wall for everyday water, tea and coffee — specified, not assumed.
Carry ringClip or carry it between the gym, desk and car without an extra handle.
Colour and logoMultiple in-stock colours plus custom colour and logo by laser, silk screen or heat transfer.
On patents: this bottle uses a conventional flip-top lid with a built-in straw, not a FreeSip-style dual sip-and-chug spout. We do not claim any product is "patent-free" — if your market has specific patent concerns, we can arrange a freedom-to-operate (FTO) review of the exact design before production and design around anything it flags.

How we control quality

Before a bulk order we agree a written specification and a signed sample. Typical acceptance checks for this bottle include a side and inverted leak test on the retail lid, a cycle test on the flip mechanism, gasket and straw inspection, and a food-contact material report on the liner. Third-party inspection to an agreed AQL can be arranged before shipment.

STWADD is a factory-backed supply company and runs a China buying office. Production is made to our specification by audited partner factories, and we say plainly which part is our operation and which is our partners'.
