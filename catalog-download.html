Common questions

Does the catalogue include prices?

No. The PDF contains product specifications, colours, capacities, MOQ and carton information only. Prices are quoted per order after we confirm the model, quantity and destination.

How do I save the catalogue to my phone?

Open your phone camera, point it at the QR code and tap the link. Most browsers will offer to download or save the PDF. You can also tap the Download PDF button.

What products are in the 2027 catalogue?

The 39-page catalogue covers mugs, tumblers and bottles: stainless steel tumblers, coffee mugs, car cups, sport bottles and related drinkware, with model numbers, materials, capacities, colours, MOQ and packing details.

Can I get a quotation?

Yes. Send the item numbers, your quantity and destination port, and we will reply with pricing. OEM/ODM and private label projects are welcome.
