Pantone Color Finder

Search Pantone C (Coated) and U (Uncoated) color codes, or pick any color to find its closest Pantone reference. Built for selecting and confirming custom water bottle colors.

⚠️ Important note: Pantone® color values shown here are unofficial, approximate screen representations compiled from public color libraries. Pantone does not publish official RGB/HEX values. Monitor, lighting and material (coated vs uncoated, stainless steel, powder coat, plastic) all shift the final look.
