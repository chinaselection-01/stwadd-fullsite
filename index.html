What "your factory" actually means with STWADD

QC people on the floor

Our own QC staff work the production line daily — first article, in-process checks and the final AQL 2.5 inspection before the container is sealed.

Your tooling, your call

You pay for the mould, so the mould is yours. We hold it, maintain it, and hand it over with a signed record on request.

Sample room & R&D

Over 50 new designs a year, 2D/3D drawings for approval before tooling, and signed NDAs on your artwork. Samples in 5-10 days.

Production scheduling

We plan the run, chase the line, and take responsibility for what leaves — 20-35 days for custom production, 3-7 days for ready stock.

We do not own the plant — we own the process. That is the honest version, and it is the version that survives an audit: we qualify the factory, station our people in it, hold the specification, and sign for the container. You are welcome to visit the factory in Yongkang with us, or to audit the line that makes your order before it ships.

FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

What types of water bottles do you manufacture?+
We manufacture a wide range of drinkware including insulated stainless steel bottles, tumblers, coffee mugs, beer growlers, wine flasks, plastic water bottles, sports bottles, kids bottles, lunch boxes, and cooler bags. All products support custom OEM/ODM services.
What is your minimum order quantity (MOQ)?+
MOQ is flexible and can be negotiated based on product type and customization requirements. For standard products with simple logo printing, MOQ can be as low as 500-1000 pieces. For fully customized new molds, we'll discuss based on your specific needs.
Can I get samples before placing a bulk order?+
Yes, absolutely! Sample lead time is typically 5-7 working days. Samples are charged but will be refunded when you place a bulk order. We also offer free samples for qualified long-term partners.
What certifications do your products have?+
Our products are certified by SGS, TUV, FDA, LFGB, and comply with BPA-free standards. We hold ISO 9001 quality management system certification. All materials meet international food-grade safety standards.
How long is the production lead time?+
Standard orders take 25-35 days after deposit received. Rush orders can be expedited to 15-20 days depending on product complexity and current production schedule. Sample orders ship within 5-7 days.
If I provide my sketches/design, can you sign an NDA?+
Yes, we take intellectual property protection very seriously. We're happy to sign a Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA) before receiving any confidential designs or information. Your designs will be fully protected throughout our cooperation.
Are you a factory or a trading company?+
We are a factory-backed supply company, not a reseller. STWADD works with audited partner production lines in Yongkang, Zhejiang, China — the drinkware manufacturing hub of East Asia — with in-house mold making, logo printing and assembly. This lets us offer cheap, factory-direct prices with no middleman markup.
Do you accept OEM/ODM orders from other brands, wholesalers or trading companies?+
Yes. We are an OEM/ODM factory and regularly manufacture for other companies in the trade. We accept bulk B2B orders from brands, wholesalers, distributors and trading companies under their private label (white-label), protect designs with an NDA, and keep client confidentiality.
Are your water bottles cheap / factory-direct priced?+
Yes. As a factory-direct manufacturer in Yongkang, China, we offer the lowest factory-direct prices with no trader markup — consistently among the most cost-effective sources for custom drinkware. Pricing depends on material, customization and order volume; MOQ starts around 500 pieces and is negotiable.
Are you the cheapest water bottle factory in China?+
STWADD is consistently among the most cost-effective OEM/ODM drinkware factories for bulk B2B buyers. Working the factory floor in Yongkang — the drinkware cluster of East Asia — with audited partner factories running automated lines and mature local supply chains, we pass the cost-effective factory-direct prices to buyers with zero middleman markup. The larger your order, the lower the unit cost.
Do you also run a China buying office / sourcing agent for retail chains?+
Yes. Beyond our drinkware supply line, STWADD operates an AI-powered China buying office / sourcing agent for supermarket chains and retail buyers. Our human-led, AI-assisted sourcing matches suppliers, automates RFQs and screens compliance, then we find products across categories — drinkware (from our audited partner factories in Yongkang), kitchenware & dining, home storage & organization, cleaning & household care, pet supplies, toys & baby, home textiles, beauty tools, garden & outdoor, electronics & lighting, hardware & tools, and seasonal & party merchandise — consolidate multiple suppliers into one container, perform AQL 2.5 quality inspection and factory audits, handle private-label packaging, and arrange freight to your port with a transparent 3-5% commission and no hidden trading-company markup. See our AI China Buying Office page for the full service.
