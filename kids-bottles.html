Frequently Asked Questions

What lid types are available for kids?

We offer sippy, straw and spout lids with one-hand open mechanisms suitable for toddlers and school-age children.

Are kids bottles BPA-free?

Yes, all kids bottles use BPA-free Tritan or 304 stainless with food-safe, non-toxic coatings.

Can they go in the dishwasher?

Lids are top-rack dishwasher safe; we provide care labels for each design.
