Frequently Asked Questions

Is the stainless steel you use 304 or 316?

Inner walls are 18/8 304 as standard; 316 is offered for premium anti-corrosion requests such as citrus or seawater use.

Are these bottles dishwasher safe?

Most powder-coated bodies are hand-wash recommended; lids are generally top-rack dishwasher safe. We label care guidance per SKU.

Do you provide material certificates?

Yes — mill test reports, FDA/LFGB and SGS certificates are provided with every shipment.
