How STWADD sources Garden & Outdoor for your chain

  1. Find & vet suppliers — we shortlist 5-10 vetted factories per item and verify credentials, capacity and compliance.
  2. RFQ & quote compare — our AI drafts RFQs, collects replies, and compares price, MOQ, lead time and landed cost side by side.
  3. Consolidate — goods from multiple suppliers are received, inspected and loaded into one FCL at our warehouse.
  4. Inspect — pre-production sample approval plus pre-shipment AQL 2.5 inspection with a downloadable report.
  5. Private label — your logo, retail-ready boxes, barcode (UPC/EAN) placement and gift-set assembly.
  6. Ship — we arrange ocean freight, documents (FDA/LFGB where needed) and destination-port clearance. FOB / EXW supported.
