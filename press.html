1. How we label coverage — and what we currently have

Supplier websites tend to blur three very different things: genuine editorial reporting, a creator's video, and the company's own announcements. We keep them apart, because a buyer deserves to know which is which.

Our rules for this page

  • We only list coverage we can link to the original. No screenshots of a headline, no "as featured in" without a URL.
  • Every record is typed: editorial media coverage, creator & video coverage, or company announcement.
  • We state who published it. If we published it ourselves, we say so on the record.
  • We state what the coverage does not prove — see the note under each record.
  • We do not buy coverage, and we do not publish customer testimonials without written permission.

Short boilerplate — 40 words

Yongkang STWADD Houseware Co., Ltd. is a factory-backed drinkware supply company in Yongkang, Zhejiang, China, producing insulated bottles, tumblers, flasks and mugs to buyers' specification through audited partner factories, and running a China buying office for retail chains.

Medium boilerplate — 100 words

Yongkang STWADD Houseware Co., Ltd., established in 2017 in Yongkang, Zhejiang, is a factory-backed supply company for insulated drinkware. It works directly with audited partner factories in China's drinkware manufacturing hub, controlling specification, tooling, quality control and delivery on buyers' behalf, and accepts OEM and ODM bulk orders from brands, wholesalers, distributors and trading companies. Its 2026 catalogue covers 192 models, with ready stock dispatching in 7–15 days and custom production in 25–30 days. It also runs a China buying office for supermarket and retail buyers.

Long boilerplate — 180 words

Yongkang STWADD Houseware Co., Ltd. was established in 2017 in Yongkang, Zhejiang, the centre of China's drinkware manufacturing industry. The company describes itself as a factory-backed supply company rather than a reseller: it qualifies and works directly with audited partner factories, and holds each production run to the buyer's own approved specification rather than brokering orders onward without disclosure. Its range covers insulated stainless steel bottles, vacuum flasks, 20oz and 40oz tumblers, car cups, coffee and travel mugs, wine and spirits decanters and BPA-free Tritan plastic bottles — 192 models in the 2026 catalogue, customisable from 10 pieces on existing models, with tooling from 3,000 pieces for new shapes. Materials are food-grade 304 and 316 stainless steel, lead-free and PFAS-free, with RCS-certified recycled 316 available. Testing is by SGS and TUV, and the company holds ISO 9001. STWADD exhibits at international trade shows including HOMELIFE Indonesia in Jakarta, and operates a China buying office serving retail chains with supplier matching, AQL 2.5 inspection, private label and container consolidation.
